# Quick Start: Using the Dashboard

## 🚀 Getting Started

Your MovingTasks app now has a **Dashboard** that provides instant insights into your moving progress!

## 📱 Navigation

After the splash screen, you'll see a **tab bar** at the bottom with two options:

1. **📊 Dashboard** - Your analytics hub (opens by default)
2. **📝 Tasks** - Your task management interface

Simply tap between tabs to switch views!

## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Quick Stats (Top Cards)
Three cards showing at a glance:
- **Total Tasks**: How many tasks you've created
- **Completed**: How many you've finished ✅
- **Pending**: How many are left to do ⏳

### 2. Overall Progress Bar
- Shows your completion percentage
- Visual progress indicator in green
- Updates automatically as you complete tasks

### 3. Financial Summary 💰
*Appears when you have task items*
- Total number of items
- How many you've purchased
- Total money spent
- Great for budget tracking!

### 4. Priority Breakdown 🚩
See how your tasks are distributed:
- **High Priority** (Red) - Urgent tasks
- **Medium Priority** (Orange) - Important but not urgent
- **Low Priority** (Green) - Can wait

### 5. Location Breakdown 📍
Where are your tasks?
- Kitchen, Bedroom, Bathroom, etc.
- Helps you plan room-by-room

### 6. Category Breakdown 📁
What type of work?
- Cleaning, Painting, Organizing, etc.
- See what kind of tasks dominate your list

### 7. High Priority Tasks 🔥
*Appears when you have high-priority tasks*
- Quick list of your most urgent tasks
- Shows location and category at a glance
- Up to 5 tasks shown

### 8. Recently Completed ✅
*Appears when you've completed tasks*
- Celebrate your progress!
- See your last 5 completed tasks
- Great motivation booster

## 💡 Tips

### First Time User?
1. Tap the **Tasks** tab
2. Tap the **More** button (three dots)
3. Select **Add Sample Data**
4. Go back to **Dashboard** tab
5. Explore all the sections with sample data!

### Adding Your Own Data
1. Go to **Tasks** tab
2. Tap the **+** button
3. Fill in task details
4. Add task items with prices
5. Return to **Dashboard** to see updated stats

### Best Practices
- ✅ Set priorities accurately for better insights
- ✅ Add task items with prices for budget tracking
- ✅ Mark items as purchased to track spending
- ✅ Complete tasks to watch your progress grow!
- ✅ Check Dashboard regularly for motivation

## 🎨 Color Coding

Learn the color system:
- 🔴 **Red/Orange** = High Priority / Urgent
- 🟠 **Orange** = Medium Priority / Important
- 🟢 **Green** = Low Priority / Completed

## 📈 Watching Your Progress

The dashboard updates **automatically** when you:
- ✅ Add new tasks
- ✅ Complete tasks
- ✅ Purchase items
- ✅ Change priorities
- ✅ Update categories or locations

No refresh needed - it's always current!

## 🎯 Using Dashboard Insights

### Planning Your Day
1. Check **High Priority Tasks**
2. Note the **Location Breakdown**
3. Plan to work on tasks in one location at a time

### Budget Management
1. View **Financial Summary**
2. See how much you've spent
3. Plan upcoming purchases

### Motivation
1. Watch your **Progress Bar** fill up
2. Celebrate in **Recently Completed**
3. See your **Completed** count grow

### Organization
1. Use **Category Breakdown** to see task types
2. Use **Location Breakdown** to plan room-by-room
3. Use **Priority Breakdown** to balance your workload

## 🔄 Workflow Example

**Morning Routine:**
1. Open app → Dashboard loads
2. Check **High Priority Tasks**
3. Note **Financial Summary** for shopping needs
4. Switch to **Tasks** tab
5. Work on high-priority items
6. Mark tasks complete as you finish
7. Return to **Dashboard** to see progress!

**Planning Session:**
1. Check **Priority Breakdown** - Too many high priority? Re-evaluate!
2. Check **Location Breakdown** - Can you batch tasks by room?
3. Check **Category Breakdown** - Need to hire help for certain types?
4. Check **Financial Summary** - On budget or over?
